The Earthroot Story: A Passion for Mycelium

Earthroot Mushrooms was born out of a profound respect for nature's quietest powerhouse — the mushroom. Situated in Pullurampara, Thiruvambady, Kozhikode, our farm bridges modern environmental science with artisanal passion.

Unlike mass-manufactured produce, every single cluster of Earthroot mushrooms is nurtured in clean micro-climates, hydrated with purified air mist, and harvested at exact peak maturity for rich umami flavor and maximum bio-availability.

Transparent Cultivation

From Spore to Table Process

Take a glimpse inside our natural 4-step sustainable growing methodology.

01

Organic Preparation

We source pure organic agricultural biomass and steam-sterilize it at high temperature to create a pristine substrate free of pathogens.

02

Spawning & Mycelium

High-vigor pure mushroom culture (spawn) is introduced under sterile laminar air flow, encouraging dense mycelial network development.

03

Climate Fruiting

Bags are placed in humidity and oxygen-controlled fruiting rooms where perfect ambient light triggers rapid, healthy mushroom blooming.

04

Morning Harvest

Mushrooms are individually hand-picked at peak caps size, gently sorted into ventilated packages, and dispatched directly.
